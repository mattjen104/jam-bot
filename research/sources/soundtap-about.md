# About soundtap

A directory of live college and community radio, built so you can hear what a real person is playing right now.

## Why this exists

Algorithmic radio is very good at giving you more of what you already like. College and community radio is good at the opposite. Somewhere right now a volunteer is three hours into a shift, playing a record you would never have been served.

soundtap collects those stations in one place, works out what is on air at this moment across every timezone they broadcast in, and gets you there in one click.

## What a tap is

A tap is the only vote on the site. One per listener per broadcast — tap a show tonight and you can tap it again next week. It is not a like and it does not train anything. It is a way of telling the next listener that something was worth stopping for.

## Where the audio comes from

Nothing is rehosted. When you press play, your browser connects straight to the station’s own stream, exactly as it would from their site. Rights in the programming stay with the broadcasters, and the listener count is theirs.

Every stream is re-checked automatically. A station that stops answering is dimmed here rather than handed to you as a player that never starts.

## Non-commercial only

Every station carried is licensed non-commercial — student, community, or volunteer-run. No commercial broadcasters, and no advertising here either.
