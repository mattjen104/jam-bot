---
name: Nostr collection publishing gate
description: Conditions that must be met before reconsidering Nostr collection publishing or curator payments.
---

Nostr playlist publishing and curator zaps are deferred. Do not select an event
convention or include Nostr work in the BYOM/JSPF collection-page milestone
until an external reader has consumed Lore's canonical ordered and unresolved
collection entries.

**Why:** Selecting a protocol before another client can read Lore's collection
model risks creating a private dialect whose interoperability has not been
proven.

**How to apply:** After a working reader exists, require a separate review of a
public-artifact contract covering publication, replacement, deletion, privacy,
and interoperability before considering event formats, key management, relays,
read-back, zaps, or artist payment routing.
