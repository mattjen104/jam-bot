---
name: Lore station culling
description: Editorial and safety rules for pruning Lore's station catalogue.
---

Cull stations by evidence, not by category size. Soft-hide clear algorithmic/background brands and redundant Radio Browser rows that resolve to an exact duplicate stream. Prefer a curated canonical row; otherwise prefer the healthy copy with metadata and stronger directory evidence. Preserve station and spin history.

Never automatically cull Core, Campus, Public & Community, or Independent DJ stations solely because they currently lack metadata or spin history.

**Why:** Lore's value comes from human selectors and recoverable community sources. A missing feed integration can be repaired, while duplicate directory variants and generic mood channels add noise without distinct editorial value.

**How to apply:** Keep culls reversible through `hidden=true`. Apply permanent discovery blocks only to unambiguously off-mission brands. Review human-curated stations individually before hiding them.
