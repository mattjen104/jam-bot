---
name: Transcript identity grounding
description: Rules for admitting spoken DJ and show identities as evidence.
---

An exact transcript span proves only what words were recognized, not that those words name a DJ or show. Admit identity claims only when the same segment contains an unambiguous role or program introduction; ordinary “this is…” and “I’m…” clauses remain claimless.

**Why:** Real pilot speech promoted “saying,” “what you gotta,” and “the werewolf song” as DJ identities. Exact-span validation and a generic-name denylist could not detect that semantic error.

**How to apply:** Preserve transcript segments even when no identity is admitted, require cue grammar again during claim validation as defense in depth, and version extractor provenance whenever admission semantics change.
