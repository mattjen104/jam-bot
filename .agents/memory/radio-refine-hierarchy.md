---
name: Radio refine hierarchy
description: Why Radio targeting scopes are secondary and how their active state should surface.
---

Radio defaults to the listener's entire library. Inbox/Rotation/Shelf targeting and one-artist targeting are secondary, mutually exclusive ways to narrow what Radio listens for; keep them together under **Refine**, not as a permanent top-level lens row.

**Why:** These modes become useful only after a library has structure and are minor compared with the Radio/Inbox surface choice. Giving each a persistent pill made navigation, scope, filters, and sorting look equally important.

**How to apply:** Keep the crossing-window selector hidden. Expose sorting as a floating icon menu and Refine as a floating sliders/abacus icon with an active-count badge. Put scope, artist focus, age, station categories, and followed-only under Radio’s Refine panel. Never show the artist finder at the top of Inbox/Library. Surface only a non-default Radio scope as a removable chip; clearing it restores the entire library.
