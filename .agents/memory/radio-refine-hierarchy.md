---
name: Radio refine hierarchy
description: Why Radio targeting scopes are secondary and how their active state should surface.
---

Radio defaults to the listener's entire library. Inbox/Rotation/Shelf targeting and one-artist targeting are secondary, mutually exclusive ways to narrow what Radio listens for; keep them together in the visible Radio refinement panel.

**Why:** These controls need to be immediately usable while tuning Radio, but they are parameters of Radio rather than top-level Library destinations. A visible grouped panel matches that hierarchy better than a hidden popover or separate lens row.

**How to apply:** Keep the crossing-window and track-age controls hidden. In the Listen for panel show Entire library, Rotation, Shelf, and Single artist as peer filters; place station Filters, Sort, and followed-only there too. On the active Discover screen, put the Radio submode directly below this panel. Ranked is the full list; Presets reuses the compact remote view until actual preset/Perrin behavior is designed.
