---
name: Radio refine hierarchy
description: Why Radio targeting scopes are secondary and how their active state should surface.
---

Radio uses the listener's full library by default. Its visible control bar contains only artist targeting, station filters, and sorting.

**Why:** Library workflow scopes and followed-only filtering made Radio’s controls too dense and duplicated the permanent navigation. The remaining controls need to be immediately usable while tuning Radio.

**How to apply:** Keep the control bar to one line in this order: Find artists, Filters, Sort. Do not show a Listen for label, Entire library/Rotation/Shelf scopes, followed-only, crossing-window, or track-age controls. Ranked is the full list; Presets reuses the compact remote view until actual preset/Perrin behavior is designed.
