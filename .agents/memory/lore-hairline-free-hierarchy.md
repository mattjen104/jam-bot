name: Lore hairline-free hierarchy
description: Visual hierarchy convention for Lore surfaces without persistent one-pixel rules or control outlines.
---

Avoid persistent hairline dividers and one-pixel control outlines in Lore UI.

**Why:** The product uses typesetting rather than thin rules to establish structure.

**How to apply:** Separate regions with spacing, type size and weight, casing, alignment, and restrained tonal fills. Keep keyboard-only focus outlines where accessibility requires them.
