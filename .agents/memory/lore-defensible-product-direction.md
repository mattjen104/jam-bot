---
name: Lore defensible product direction
description: Product-strategy boundary for prioritizing Lore after the competitive review.
---

Lead with a paid radio-to-record utility: human-curated discovery becomes a durable provenance receipt, album investigation, artist-support route, and provider handoff/export. Treat portable identity as supporting infrastructure rather than a standalone network.

The only compatible secondary wedge is a small invite room anchored to a recorded radio run or album investigation. Do not expand it into a public social graph or cross-provider synchronized player before the primary loop proves repeat use and willingness to pay.

**Why:** Lore's difficult-to-copy asset is trustworthy station, show, selector, and time provenance. Universal playback, generic social music, broad AI companionship, and album browsing are stronger incumbent markets with higher provider and operating risk.

**How to apply:** New listener-facing work should strengthen crossing → keep → album → investigate → launch/support/replay. Prefer standards and handoffs at provider boundaries; archive work that cannot reinforce this loop or a validated evidence-room moment.
