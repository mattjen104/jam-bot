---
name: Lore crossing row hierarchy
description: The display hierarchy for live crossing rows in the Dial.
---

Crossing rows are scan-oriented: the matched artist is the primary signal, the human DJ supplies the sentence subject when available, and the station is a stable tertiary byline rather than repeated in the sentence. A valid show may precede the station in that byline.

**Why:** Every row already has a station, so repeating it in each sentence adds width without helping listeners choose. The artist is the changing fact that explains why a station surfaced.

**How to apply:** Suppress song titles only when crossing evidence drives the row. Keep title-inclusive now-playing copy for ordinary non-crossing rows. Highlight matched artist names individually; keep DJ, show, station, labels, and punctuation neutral. Omit placeholder shows and avoid dangling optional phrases.
