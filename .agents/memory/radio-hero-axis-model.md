---
name: Radio hero axis model
description: The interaction and information hierarchy chosen for Lore Radio's front-door station heroes.
---

The front door uses one dominant axis per information layer: station heroes snap vertically and lifetime crossing covers scroll horizontally. The station title surface is itself the horizontal station rail: the selected station is reordered to the far-left position as a much larger square, followed by smaller square station tiles. Station identity and Now Playing remain the primary information; crossing history is secondary context below playback.

**Why:** A separate remote or a vertical remote beside the hero creates competing navigation systems and makes the listening room feel like a dashboard. Making the title surface the rail keeps station identity and direct selection in one place while preserving orthogonal station and album gestures.

**How to apply:** Keep station tiles visually limited to identity/active state. Reorder the selected tile to the far left and enlarge it substantially. Use icon-only chevrons outside the rail—never “Previous”/“Next” text buttons. Synchronize hero position and rail selection without starting playback. Load large crossing histories on demand rather than bloating every Dial response.
