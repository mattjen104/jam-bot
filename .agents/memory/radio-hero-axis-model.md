---
name: Radio hero axis model
description: The interaction and information hierarchy chosen for Lore Radio's front-door station heroes.
---

The front door uses one dominant axis per information layer: station heroes snap vertically, lifetime crossing covers scroll horizontally inside each hero, and the station remote remains a compact horizontal mini-map. Station identity and Now Playing belong together at the top of each hero; crossing history is secondary context below playback.

**Why:** A vertical remote beside a vertical hero feed creates two competing navigation systems and makes the listening room feel like a dashboard. Orthogonal station and album gestures are easier to distinguish, while the remote gives direct access without duplicating hero metadata.

**How to apply:** Keep remote buttons visually limited to station identity/active state. Synchronize vertical hero position, Previous/Next controls, and the remote selection without starting playback. Load large crossing histories on demand rather than bloating every Dial response.
