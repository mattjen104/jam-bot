---
name: On-air identity phrases
description: Real host and show identity often appears relationally rather than as a formal station identification.
---

Treat relational phrases such as “our co-host for [show]” and “[person] and I started this show” as useful grounded identity evidence when the named value is supported by an official station source.

**Why:** A live KEXP capture explicitly referred to a co-host, another host, and Sounds of Survivance, but did not use the narrow “you’re listening to” or “my name is” forms. An extractor limited to formal introductions missed the credible evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve the exact transcript span, use the active official schedule/show page to resolve ASR spelling noise, and distinguish a named co-host from the speaking host. Do not infer the speaker’s own identity from “me and [other host].”
