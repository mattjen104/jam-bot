---
name: Mockup graduation visibility
description: How to avoid leaving an approved mockup invisible or only partially reflected in Lore production.
---

A mockup source file, its canvas iframe, and the production app are three separate things. A production style tweak does not make the original mockup visible or faithfully implemented.

**Why:** The compact tree study existed in the mockup sandbox but was not pinned as a canvas frame, while the home page retained a functional but visually different two-band treatment. The user could therefore neither find the exact reference nor recognize it in the app.

**How to apply:** When a prototype is the intended design, ensure its iframe is live and presented on the canvas, then graduate its composition, tokens, typography, responsive layout, and interaction grammar into the production surface while retaining real app data and controls.
