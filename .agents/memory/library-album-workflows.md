---
name: Library album workflows
description: Library workflow tabs are album states, not alternate song and artist views.
---

Library is organized only by album workflows: Inbox, Rotation, Shelf, Passed, and Unresolved. Do not mix Songs or Artists grouping navigation into this header.

The workflow row begins with a round Lore moon disclosure. Clicking it reveals or hides a quieter row for the separate Radio, Press, and Merch sections; that row stays open inside those sections.

**Why:** Inbox and its sibling tabs describe an album’s workflow state. Combining those states with Songs/Artists grouping made two unrelated navigation models compete in one control area.

**How to apply:** Keep workflow pages album-only and free of search/grouping controls. Canonicalize old song, artist, focus, and search URL state back to the selected album workflow. Keep Radio, Press, and Merch in the moon-controlled secondary row rather than mixing them with album states.
