---
name: Library album workflows
description: Library workflow tabs are album states, not alternate song and artist views.
---

Library is organized only by album workflows: Inbox, Rotation, Shelf, Passed, and Unresolved. Do not mix Songs or Artists grouping navigation into this header.

The Lore moon occupies its own brand row. The next row contains album workflows and the Discover mode; Discover’s first submode is Radio. Press and Merch stay hidden for now.

**Why:** Inbox and its sibling tabs describe an album’s workflow state. Combining those states with Songs/Artists grouping made two unrelated navigation models compete in one control area.

**How to apply:** Keep workflow pages album-only and free of search/grouping controls. Canonicalize old song, artist, focus, and search URL state back to the selected album workflow. Keep the moon above navigation; selecting Discover reveals its submode row, beginning with Radio. Selecting an album workflow closes Discover.
