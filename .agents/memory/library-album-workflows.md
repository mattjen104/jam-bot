---
name: Library album workflows
description: Library workflow tabs are album states, not alternate song and artist views.
---

Library is organized only by album workflows: Inbox, Rotation, Shelf, Passed, and Unresolved. Do not mix Songs or Artists grouping navigation into this header.

The Lore moon occupies its own brand row. The next row has exactly two main toggles in this order: Discover, Inbox. Discover’s first submode is Radio; Inbox owns the album-workflow submodes. Press and Merch stay hidden.

**Why:** Inbox and its sibling tabs describe an album’s workflow state. Combining those states with Songs/Artists grouping made two unrelated navigation models compete in one control area.

**How to apply:** Keep workflow pages album-only and free of search/grouping controls. Canonicalize old song, artist, focus, and search URL state back to the selected album workflow. Keep the moon above navigation. Show Discover left of Inbox as the only main toggles. Under Discover show Radio; under Inbox show Rotation, Shelf, Passed, and Unresolved.
