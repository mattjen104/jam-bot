---
name: Station identity evidence gates
description: Accuracy rules for listener-facing station characterizations derived from Lore spin and first-party data.
---

Listener-facing station identity copy must be grounded primarily in a fresh rolling sample of resolved spins, with minimum unique-track and unique-artist breadth plus enough genre-tagged plays to support every named genre. A large resolved-spin count or an existing cumulative genre profile is not sufficient by itself.

Reject or ignore homepage excerpts that are navigation text, titles, slogans without descriptive content, or truncated fragments. Likewise, exclude “artists” that are station IDs, host/show labels, placeholders, or parser artifacts. First-party descriptions establish mission or location only; they do not establish what the station currently plays.

**Why:** A station-by-station audit found that a mechanical gate based on resolved-spin count, any homepage blurb, and any genre profile materially overstated publishable coverage. Several qualifying feeds had only a handful of genre-tagged recordings or polluted metadata despite hundreds of logged spins.

**How to apply:** For any station summary, recommendation, or generated sentence, use recent observed plays as the primary evidence, keep source/freshness/sample counts auditable, use cautious language for thin enrichment, and prefer “not enough evidence” over generic copy.
