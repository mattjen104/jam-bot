---
name: Explore surface boundary
description: Lore's Explore surface is for actionable live discovery, while durable Press and Shows belong in Stack/Library.
---

Explore is not a chronological activity feed. It should help the listener choose the next broadcast: live crossings first, then worthwhile live rooms, with scan and small recent-trail support. Press, shows, and full history are durable library material and should not become primary Explore lanes.

**Why:** A single feed made live radio, saved music, editorial content, and archives compete as if they were the same job. The listener needs a surface for deciding what to hear next, while Stack needs to remember things.

**How to apply:** Keep `/feed` as the stable route but present it as Explore. Preserve playback and live ranking; when Press/Shows move, migrate their access into Library/Stack rather than deleting the underlying data or leaving them as equal-weight Explore tabs.
