---
name: Ambient is music-only
description: Catalogue rule separating musical ambient radio from non-music utility audio.
---

The listener-facing category is named **Ambient** and includes musical ambient, drone, and atmospheric stations only. Sleep aids, nature sounds, rain sounds, and white-noise utility streams must not appear in the category or be admitted by station discovery. Preserve existing utility rows as soft-hidden rather than deleting their history.

**Why:** The product is a music-discovery radio experience; utility sound streams dilute the station catalogue and do not belong beside musical ambient programming.

**How to apply:** Keep musical ambient stations eligible for the Ambient pool. Treat obvious non-music utility names as permanent discovery exclusions, and ensure migrations remove any existing matches from the ambient pool while retaining their rows hidden.
