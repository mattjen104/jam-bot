---
name: Support-route schedule priority
description: Editorial priority and extraction rule for improving non-Spinitron station schedules.
---

Prioritize schedule refresh and structured-source research for stations that already have a verified donation/support or store route.

**Why:** Better show and DJ attribution should first benefit stations listeners can directly support. Structured first-party grids also avoid LLM omissions and reduce repeated interpretive scraping.

**How to apply:** Within the normal fair retry queue, process support/store stations before other schedule targets. Prefer a validated JSON, ICS, CMS, or deterministic server-rendered weekly grid over LLM extraction, while continuing to honor robots rules, timeouts, and host pacing.
